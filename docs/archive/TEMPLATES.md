## Universal Design Philosophy

**Every template must:**
- ✅ Solve a problem that exists across 5+ industries
- ✅ Be adaptable with < 30 min customization
- ✅ Work standalone OR integrate with existing systems
- ✅ Have clear value proposition within 10 seconds
- ✅ Include AI enhancement opportunities

**We avoid:**
- ❌ Industry-specific jargon in core features
- ❌ Regulatory compliance built-in (customizable)
- ❌ Assumptions about existing tech stack
- ❌ Hard-coded business rules
